Any Colour You Like what I work on

  1. Energy, carbon and water

    The electricity and carbon of U.S. hyperscale data centers, and where they use water, on site and at the power plant.

  2. Health

    The air pollution from the power plants that run AI, the lives lost when coal plants stay open to feed it, and a grid planned around health.

  3. People and justice

    Who pays for AI, and what it means for the communities living next to bitcoin mines or a proposed gas plant.
