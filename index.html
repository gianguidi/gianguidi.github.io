04 / A little about me

Connecting
disciplines.
Following curiosity.

My Harvard profile ↗

I’m a Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard University, working with Francesca Dominici.

I use machine learning, data science, and statistical modeling to understand how technological change affects the environment and the people who live in it. I’m especially interested in turning large, complex datasets into evidence that can inform policy.

I earned my PhD in Artificial Intelligence through the Italian National Doctorate at the University of Pisa, under the supervision of Tiziano Squartini and Diego Garlaschelli.

Mentors & academic community +

My PhD board and supervising committee included Falco J. Bargagli-Stoffi, Dino Pedreschi, Giulio Rossetti, and Rossana Mastrandrea.
